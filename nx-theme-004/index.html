How to find your abaya length

By the Sahar studio · 9 October 2026

Abaya lengths are measured in inches, from the top of the shoulder to the hem. The right length depends on your height and on the shoes you wear most.

Measure in one minute

Stand straight in the shoes you'll wear. Ask someone to measure from the top of your shoulder, next to the neck, to the floor. Take away 1 to 2 inches so the hem just clears the ground.

Between two lengths? Choose the longer one for heels and the shorter one for flats.

Our length finder does the maths for you, and every abaya in the collection comes in 52, 54, 56, 58 and 60 inches.
