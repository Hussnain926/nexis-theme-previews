How to care for gold vermeil

Gold vermeil is sterling silver with a thick layer of gold. Treated well, it keeps its shine for years. These five habits help.

1. Last on, first off

Put jewellery on after perfume, hairspray and moisturiser, and take it off before the gym, the pool or the shower. Chemicals and chlorine wear plating fastest.

“Treat it like a silk scarf: worn every day, put away with care.”

2. Store each piece apart

Keep pieces in their pouch or a lined box so chains don't tangle and stones don't scratch softer metal.

3. Polish gently

A soft, dry cloth after wearing is all it needs. Skip silver dips and abrasive cloths: they remove gold along with tarnish.
