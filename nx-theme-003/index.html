How much should my dog eat?

By the Muddy Paws team · 12 October 2026

Most dogs do best on two meals a day. The right amount depends on three things: their weight, their age and how active they are.

A simple starting point

Dog weightCalmActive
5 kg85 g105 g
10 kg140 g175 g
20 kg230 g290 g
30 kg310 g390 g
Check your dog's ribs: you should feel them easily but not see them. Adjust by 10% at a time.

Puppies need more food for their size, and older dogs usually need a little less. If in doubt, ask your vet.
