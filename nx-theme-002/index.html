Grown by smallholders around Pitalito in Huila and washed at a shared mill. Sweet and easy-going: great as a filter, and it holds up well with milk.

The perfect V60, step by step

By the Ashgrove roasting team · 14 October 2026

The V60 is the simplest way to taste what makes a coffee special. It rewards a little care, and once you have the rhythm it takes three minutes.

What you need

A V60 dripper and paper filter, 15 g of freshly ground coffee (medium-fine, like table salt), 250 ml of water just off the boil and a scale with a timer.

CupsCoffeeWaterTime
115 g250 ml2:45
230 g500 ml3:30
345 g750 ml4:15

The pour

Rinse the filter, add the coffee and pour 40 ml to bloom. Wait 40 seconds, then pour slowly in circles to 150 ml, pause, and finish at 250 ml by two minutes. Let it drain.

Taste sour? Grind finer. Taste bitter? Grind coarser. Change one thing at a time.

Try it with Huila or Yirgacheffe this week: both are roasted for filter and show off their fruit and sweetness.
